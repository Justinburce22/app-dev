# Titanic (1997)

## My Favorite Movie

**Titanic** is an *epic romance and disaster film* directed by **James Cameron**. It tells a fictional love story set aboard the real RMS Titanic during its tragic maiden voyage in 1912.

---

## Quick Facts

| Detail | Information |
|--------|-------------|
| Director | James Cameron |
| Release Date | December 19, 1997 |
| Genre | Romance / Drama |
| Runtime | 194 minutes |
| Music | James Horner |

## Main Cast

1. Leonardo DiCaprio as Jack Dawson
2. Kate Winslet as Rose DeWitt Bukater
3. Billy Zane as Cal Hockley
4. Kathy Bates as Molly Brown

## Why I Love It

- Beautiful and emotional love story
- Amazing sets and visual effects
- Unforgettable soundtrack
  - "My Heart Will Go On" by Celine Dion
  - Instrumental score by James Horner
